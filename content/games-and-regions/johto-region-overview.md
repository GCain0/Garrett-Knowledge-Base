# The Johto Region

Johto is the region introduced in Generation II and is known for its deep cultural roots, ancient towers, and strong connection to nature. It sits west of Kanto and shares several traditions and Pokémon species.

## Geography

Johto features:

- Peaceful countryside towns  
- Historic temples and towers  
- Dense forests  
- Mountain passes  
- Lakes and natural landmarks  

## Major Cities

- **New Bark Town** — Home of Professor Elm.  
- **Violet City** — Flying-type Gym and Sprout Tower.  
- **Goldenrod City** — The largest city in Johto.  
- **Ecruteak City** — Legendary towers and Ghost-type Gym.

## Native Pokémon

- Chikorita  
- Cyndaquil  
- Totodile  
- Togepi  
- Mareep  

## Cultural Significance

Johto emphasizes tradition, mythology, and harmony with nature. Many legendary Pokémon originate from ancient Johto lore.
