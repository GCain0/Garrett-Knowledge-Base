# The Kanto Region

Kanto is the first region introduced in the Pokémon series and home to the original 151 Pokémon. It features iconic cities, memorable characters, and the classic starter trio: Bulbasaur, Charmander, and Squirtle.

## Geography

Kanto includes a variety of environments:

- Forests and grassy routes  
- Mountain ranges  
- Coastal towns  
- Caves and hidden paths  
- Large urban centers  

## Major Cities

- **Pallet Town** — Starting point for many Trainers.  
- **Pewter City** — Rock-type Gym.  
- **Cerulean City** — Water-type Gym.  
- **Vermilion City** — Home of the S.S. Anne.  
- **Saffron City** — Psychic-type Gym and Silph Co. HQ.
