# The Hoenn Region

Hoenn is the tropical region introduced in Generation III. Known for its warm climate, ocean routes, and volcanic landscapes, Hoenn features some of the most diverse environments in the Pokémon world.

## Geography

Hoenn includes:

- Vast ocean routes  
- Coral reefs  
- Rainforests  
- Volcanic mountains  
- Sandy deserts  
- Island towns  

## Major Cities

- **Littleroot Town** — Starting point for new Trainers.  
- **Rustboro City** — Rock-type Gym.  
- **Mauville City** — Electric-type Gym.  
- **Lavaridge Town** — Fire-type Gym near Mt. Chimney.  
- **Sootopolis City** — Hidden city inside a crater.

## Native Pokémon

- Treecko  
- Torchic  
- Mudkip  
- Ralts  
- Aron  
- Absol  

## Legendary Pokémon

Hoenn is home to the weather trio:

- Kyogre  
- Groudon  
- Rayquaza
