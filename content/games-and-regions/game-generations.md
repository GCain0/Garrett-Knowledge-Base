# Game Generations Explained

The Pokémon series is divided into generations, each introducing new regions, species, mechanics, and gameplay features. Understanding the generations helps organize the timeline of the games and the evolution of the franchise.

## Generation I — Kanto

- Pokémon Red, Blue, Yellow  
- Introduced the first 151 Pokémon  
- Region: Kanto  

## Generation II — Johto

- Gold, Silver, Crystal  
- Introduced breeding, day/night cycles  
- Region: Johto  

## Generation III — Hoenn

- Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald  
- Introduced abilities and natures  
- Region: Hoenn  

## Generation IV — Sinnoh

- Diamond, Pearl, Platinum  
- Introduced the physical/special split  
- Region: Sinnoh  

## Generation V — Unova

- Black, White, Black 2, White 2  
- Introduced fully animated sprites  
- Region: Unova  

## Generation VI — Kalos

- X and Y  
- Introduced Mega Evolution  
- Region: Kalos  

## Generation VII — Alola

- Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon  
- Introduced regional forms  
- Region: Alola  

## Generation VIII — Galar

- Sword and Shield  
- Introduced Dynamax and Gigantamax  
- Region: Galar  

## Generation IX — Paldea

- Scarlet and Violet  
- Introduced Terastalizing  
- Region: Paldea
