# The Unova Region

Unova is a region inspired by New York City, introduced in Generation V. It features modern cities, diverse environments, and one of the largest regional Pokédexes.

## Geography

Unova includes:

- Skyscraper-filled cities  
- Industrial zones  
- Forests and plains  
- Desert routes  
- Coastal areas  

## Major Cities

- **Nuvema Town** — Starting point.  
- **Castelia City** — Massive port city.  
- **Nimbasa City** — Amusement park and Electric-type Gym.  
- **Opelucid City** — Dragon-type Gym.

## Legendary Pokémon

- Reshiram  
- Zekrom  
- Kyurem
