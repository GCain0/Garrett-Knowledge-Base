# The Kalos Region

Kalos is a region inspired by France, introduced in Generation VI. Known for its beauty, fashion, and Mega Evolution, Kalos is one of the most elegant regions in the Pokémon world.

## Geography

Kalos features:

- Rolling countryside  
- Coastal routes  
- Snowy mountains  
- Historic castles  
- Modern cities  

## Major Cities

- **Vaniville Town** — Starting point.  
- **Lumiose City** — The largest city in the Pokémon world.  
- **Shalour City** — Home of Mega Evolution.  
- **Snowbelle City** — Ice-type Gym.

## Key Feature: Mega Evolution

Kalos introduced Mega Evolution, a temporary transformation that boosts a Pokémon’s power.
