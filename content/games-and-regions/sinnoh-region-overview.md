# The Sinnoh Region

Sinnoh is a cold, mountainous region introduced in Generation IV. Known for its deep lore, ancient ruins, and legendary Pokémon tied to time and space, Sinnoh is one of the most story-rich regions in the Pokémon world.

## Geography

Sinnoh features:

- Snowy routes  
- Mountain ranges  
- Lakes and wetlands  
- Ancient temples  
- Underground tunnels  

## Major Cities

- **Twinleaf Town** — Starting point for new Trainers.  
- **Jubilife City** — A modern, bustling metropolis.  
- **Hearthome City** — Home of contests.  
- **Snowpoint City** — Ice-type Gym.  
- **Sunyshore City** — Electric-type Gym.

## Legendary Pokémon

- Dialga (Time)  
- Palkia (Space)  
- Giratina (Distortion World)
