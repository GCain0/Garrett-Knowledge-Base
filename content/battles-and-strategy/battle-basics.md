# Battle Basics

Battling is one of the core mechanics of the Pokémon world. It blends strategy, timing, type knowledge, and teamwork between a Trainer and their Pokémon. Understanding the fundamentals of battle is essential before exploring advanced tactics or competitive play.

## How Battles Work

Pokémon battles are turn-based encounters where two or more Pokémon face off using moves. The goal is simple: reduce the opponent’s HP to zero. But the strategy behind each turn is what makes battles deep and rewarding.

### Turn Order

Turn order is determined by **Speed**.  
- Faster Pokémon act first.  
- Slower Pokémon act later.  
See: [Pokémon Stats](/pokemon-basics/pokemon-stats)

### Win Condition

A battle ends when all Pokémon on one side faint.  
Some battles allow switching, items, or special rules depending on the format.

## Core Battle Elements

### **Typing Advantage**
Type matchups determine how effective moves are.

- Fire beats Grass  
- Grass beats Water  
- Water beats Fire  

See: [Pokémon Types](/pokemon-basics/pokemon-types)

### **Move Categories**
Moves fall into three categories:

- **Physical** — Uses Attack vs. Defense  
- **Special** — Uses Special Attack vs. Special Defense  
- **Status** — Inflicts effects instead of damage  

See: [Moves and Categories](/pokemon-basics/moves-and-categories)

### **Status Conditions**
Status effects can change the flow of battle dramatically.

- **Burn** — Lowers Attack  
- **Poison** — Deals damage over time  
- **Paralysis** — Slows Speed  
- **Sleep** — Prevents action  
- **Freeze** — Immobilizes the Pokémon
