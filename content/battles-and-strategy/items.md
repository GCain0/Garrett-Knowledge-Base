# Items Overview

Items are essential tools used by Trainers throughout their journey. They support exploration, healing, evolution, and battle strategy. Understanding item categories helps Trainers navigate the world more effectively and build stronger teams.

## Healing Items

Healing items restore HP, cure status conditions, or revive fainted Pokémon. These are the most commonly used items during travel.

### Examples

- **Potion** — Restores a small amount of HP.  
- **Super Potion** — Restores more HP than a Potion.  
- **Hyper Potion** — High-level healing.  
- **Full Heal** — Cures any status condition.  
- **Revive** — Restores a fainted Pokémon to half HP.

Healing items are vital for long routes, caves, and tough battles.

## Battle Items

Battle items provide temporary boosts during combat. They can turn the tide of a difficult fight when used strategically.

### Examples

- **X Attack** — Raises Attack.  
- **X Defense** — Raises Defense.  
- **Dire Hit** — Increases critical hit chance.  
- **Guard Spec** — Prevents stat reduction.

These items are often used in Gym battles or competitive environments.

## Held Items

Held items grant passive benefits when carried by a Pokémon. They are a core part of advanced battle strategy.

### Examples

- **Leftovers** — Restores HP each turn.  
- **Choice Band** — Boosts Attack but locks move choice.  
- **Focus Sash** — Prevents one-hit knockouts.  
- **Black Sludge** — Heals Poison-types, harms others.

Held items define roles and strategies in competitive play.

## Evolution Items

Some Pokémon require special items to evolve. These items unlock new forms and abilities.

### Examples

- **Fire Stone**  
- **Water Stone**  
- **Leaf Stone**  
- **Thunder Stone**  
- **Moon Stone**

Example: Pikachu evolves into Raichu using a **Thunder Stone
