# Abilities

Abilities are passive traits that influence how a Pokémon behaves in battle. Every Pokémon species has one or more possible abilities, and some have hidden abilities that offer powerful advantages.

## What Abilities Do

Abilities activate automatically and can affect:

- Weather interactions  
- Move effectiveness  
- Status conditions  
- Stat changes  
- Battle mechanics  

Abilities add depth to strategy and can define a Pokémon’s role on a team.

## Common Abilities

### **Overgrow**
Boosts Grass-type moves when HP is low.  
Used by: [Bulbasaur](/pokemon-species/bulbasaur), [Ivysaur](/pokemon-species/ivysaur), [Venusaur](/pokemon-species/venusaur)

### **Blaze**
Boosts Fire-type moves when HP is low.  
Used by: [Charmander](/pokemon-species/charmander)

### **Static**
May paralyze opponents on contact.  
Used by: Pikachu

### **Levitate**
Grants immunity to Ground-type moves.

### **Intimidate**
Lowers the opponent’s Attack when entering battle.

## Hidden Abilities

Some Pokémon have rare hidden abilities that dramatically change their battle potential.

Examples:

- **Chlorophyll** — Doubles Speed in sunlight  
- **Solar Power** — Boosts Special Attack in sunlight at the cost of HP  
- **Lightning Rod** — Draws in Electric-type moves and boosts Special Attack  

## Why Abilities Matter

Abilities can:

- Define team synergy  
- Counter specific threats  
- Enhance move sets  
- Support weather-based strategies  
- Change matchups entirely
