# Charmander (#004)

Charmander is a Fire-type Pokémon known for the flame at the tip of its tail. This flame reflects Charmander’s health and emotions — burning brightly when it’s strong and flickering when it’s tired. Charmander is one of the three classic starter Pokémon from the Kanto region.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Lizard Pokémon  
- **Type:** Fire  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - Charmander → Charmeleon → Charizard

## Biology

Charmander’s tail flame is its most iconic feature. If the flame goes out, Charmander’s life is in danger, making it extremely protective of its tail. Charmander prefers warm environments and becomes more energetic
