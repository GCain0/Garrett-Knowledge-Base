# Blastoise (#009)

Blastoise is the final evolution of [Squirtle](/pokemon-species/squirtle). Its powerful water cannons can blast holes through thick steel.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Shellfish Pokémon  
- **Type:** Water  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - [Squirtle](/pokemon-species/squirtle) → [Wartortle](/pokemon-species/wartortle) → Blastoise

## Biology

Blastoise’s cannons are capable of firing water at extremely high pressure. It uses its heavy shell for defense and stability during battle.

## Notable Moves

- Hydro Pump  
- Flash Cannon  
- Aqua Jet  
- Skull Bash
