# Bulbasaur (#001)

Bulbasaur is a dual-type **Grass/Poison** Pokémon known for the plant bulb on its back, which grows as it evolves.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Seed Pokémon  
- **Type:** Grass / Poison  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - Bulbasaur → [Ivysaur](/pokemon-species/ivysaur) → [Venusaur](/pokemon-species/venusaur)

## Biology

Bulbasaur stores energy in its bulb, allowing it to perform powerful Grass-type moves. It thrives in sunny environments and often naps while photosynthesizing.

## Notable Moves

- Vine Whip  
- Razor Leaf  
- Sleep Powder  
- Solar Beam
