# Wartortle (#008)

Wartortle is the evolved form of [Squirtle](/pokemon-species/squirtle). Its fluffy tail stores air, helping it swim swiftly and maintain balance underwater.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Turtle Pokémon  
- **Type:** Water  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - [Squirtle](/pokemon-species/squirtle) → Wartortle → [Blastoise](/pokemon-species/blastoise)

## Biology

Wartortle’s tail is a symbol of longevity. It is often seen as a sign of wisdom and experience. Its ears and tail help it maneuver through water with precision.

## Notable Moves

- Water Pulse  
- Aqua Tail  
- Rapid Spin  
- Bite
