# Pikachu (#025)

Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokémon known worldwide as the mascot of the Pokémon franchise. Its cheeks store electricity that it releases in battle.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Mouse Pokémon  
- **Type:** Electric  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - Pichu → Pikachu → Raichu

## Biology

Pikachu stores electricity in its cheeks. When threatened, it releases powerful electric shocks. Pikachu is highly social and often lives in groups.

## Notable Moves

- Thunderbolt  
- Quick Attack  
- Electro Ball  
- Iron Tail  

## Related Species

- Raichu  
- Pichu
