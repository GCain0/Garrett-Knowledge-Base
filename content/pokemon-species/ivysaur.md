# Ivysaur (#002)

Ivysaur is the evolved form of [Bulbasaur](/pokemon-species/bulbasaur), continuing the growth of the plant bulb on its back as it prepares for its final evolution into [Venusaur](/pokemon-species/venusaur).

## Key Information

- **Category:** Seed Pokémon  
- **Type:** Grass / Poison  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - [Bulbasaur](/pokemon-species/bulbasaur) → Ivysaur → [Venusaur](/pokemon-species/venusaur)

## Biology

As Ivysaur matures, its legs grow stronger to support the weight of the blossoming plant on its back. When the bud begins to bloom, Ivysaur is close to evolving.

Ivysaur prefers sunny environments and becomes more active during daylight hours.

## Notable Moves

- Vine Whip  
- Razor Leaf  
- Sleep Powder  
- Solar Beam  
- Petal Dance
