# Squirtle (#007)

Squirtle is a Water-type Pokémon known for its turtle-like appearance and its ability to shoot powerful streams of water from its mouth. As one of the original Kanto starter Pokémon, Squirtle is beloved for its loyalty, defensive strength, and cool-headed personality.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Tiny Turtle Pokémon  
- **Type:** Water  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - Squirtle → Wartortle → Blastoise

## Biology

Squirtle’s shell is both a home and a defensive tool. Its rounded shape reduces water resistance, allowing Squirtle to swim quickly and maneuver with precision. When threatened, Squirtle can withdraw into its shell for protection or use it to launch spinning attacks.

Its water abilities come from specialized glands that allow it to shoot water with incredible accuracy — sometimes enough to carve grooves into rock.

## Notable Moves

- Water Gun  
- Bubble  
- Aqua Tail  
- Rapid Spin  
- Hydro Pump  

## Behavior

Squirtle is calm, clever, and often playful. It enjoys swimming, sunbathing on rocks, and forming strong bonds with its Trainer. Despite its small size, Squirtle is brave and determined, especially when defending its team.
