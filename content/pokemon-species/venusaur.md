# Venusaur (#003)

Venusaur is the final evolution of [Bulbasaur](/pokemon-species/bulbasaur) and [Ivysaur](/pokemon-species/ivysaur). Its large flower blooms fully, absorbing sunlight to power its strongest Grass-type attacks.

## Key Information

- **Category:** Seed Pokémon  
- **Type:** Grass / Poison  
- **Evolution Line:**  
  - [Bulbasaur](/pokemon-species/bulbasaur) → [Ivysaur](/pokemon-species/ivysaur) → Venusaur

## Biology

The flower on Venusaur’s back releases a pleasant aroma that can calm emotions and influence the surrounding environment. Venusaur becomes extremely powerful in sunlight, capable of unleashing devastating moves like Solar Beam.

Its sturdy body and deep roots in nature make it a symbol of balance and strength.

## Notable Moves

- Solar Beam  
- Petal Blizzard  
- Frenzy Plant  
- Sleep Powder  
- Sludge Bomb
