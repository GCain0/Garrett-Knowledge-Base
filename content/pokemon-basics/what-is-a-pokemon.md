# What Is a Pokémon?

Pokémon are creatures that inhabit the world of the Pokémon games, anime, and manga. They come in many shapes, sizes, and elemental types, each with unique abilities and behaviors.

## Core Characteristics

- **Species Identity** — Every Pokémon belongs to a specific species, such as [Bulbasaur](/pokemon-species/bulbasaur) or [Pikachu](/pokemon-species/pikachu).
- **Typing** — Pokémon have elemental types (e.g., Fire, Water, Grass) that determine strengths and weaknesses.
- **Moves** — Pokémon learn moves that allow them to battle, defend, or interact with the world.
- **Evolution** — Many Pokémon evolve into stronger forms, gaining new abilities and stats.

## Pokémon in the World

Pokémon coexist with humans, often forming partnerships with Trainers. They can be found in forests, oceans, cities, caves, and even outer space.
