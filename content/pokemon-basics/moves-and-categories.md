# Moves and Categories: How Pokémon Attack

Moves are the actions Pokémon use in battle. Every move belongs to a type and a category, and understanding these details is essential for building a strong team.

## Move Categories

There are three main categories:

### Physical Moves
Use Attack and target Defense.

### Special Moves
Use Special Attack and target Special Defense.

### Status Moves
Don’t deal damage but apply effects.
