# Pokémon Stats: How Strength Is Measured

Stats define a Pokémon’s capabilities in battle. Even two Pokémon of the same species can perform differently depending on their stat distribution. Understanding stats helps trainers make smarter choices when leveling, battling, or strategizing.

## The Six Core Stats

Every Pokémon has six primary stats:

### HP (Hit Points)
Determines how much damage a Pokémon can take.

### Attack & Special Attack
Control how much damage physical and special moves deal.

### Defense & Special Defense
Reduce incoming physical and special damage.

### Speed
Determines turn order.
