# Battle Conditions: Weather, Terrain, and Status Effects

Battle conditions add depth and unpredictability to Pokémon battles. They can boost certain types, weaken others, or apply long-lasting effects that shape the entire fight.

## Weather Effects

Weather can dramatically change battle flow:

### Rain
Boosts Water moves, weakens Fire.

### Sunlight
Boosts Fire moves, weakens Water.

### Sandstorm
Damages non-Rock, Ground, or Steel Pokémon.

## Terrain Effects

Terrain influences move power and defensive bonuses.

## Status Conditions

Burn, paralysis, poison, sleep, and freeze all affect battle performance.
