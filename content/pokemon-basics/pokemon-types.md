# Pokémon Types: The Foundation of Every Battle

Understanding Pokémon types is one of the most important fundamentals in the Pokémon world. Types determine strengths, weaknesses, resistances, and how effective certain moves will be. Whether you're building a team or analyzing a matchup, type knowledge shapes every decision.

## Core Type Concepts

There are 18 official Pokémon types, ranging from classics like Fire and Water to more specialized ones like Fairy and Steel. Each type interacts differently with others, creating a complex web of advantages.

### Offensive and Defensive Roles

A Pokémon’s type affects both the moves it uses and how it responds to attacks. For example, Fire-type moves are super effective against Grass, but Fire Pokémon take extra damage from Water.
