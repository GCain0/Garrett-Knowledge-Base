# Evolution Basics: How Pokémon Grow Stronger

Evolution is one of the most iconic mechanics in the Pokémon universe. It represents growth, transformation, and the natural progression of a Pokémon’s journey.

## Why Pokémon Evolve

Evolution typically increases a Pokémon’s stats, changes its appearance, and sometimes alters its type. This makes evolution a key part of team building and long-term strategy.

### Common Evolution Methods

Pokémon evolve through several methods:

#### Leveling Up
The most common method.

#### Evolution Stones
Fire Stone, Water Stone, Thunder Stone, etc.

#### Friendship
Some Pokémon evolve when they trust their trainer deeply.

#### Special Conditions
Time of day, location, moves known, or even holding items.
