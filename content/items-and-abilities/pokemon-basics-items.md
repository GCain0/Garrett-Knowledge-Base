# Items

Items play a crucial role in both battles and exploration. Trainers rely on items to heal their Pokémon, boost stats, evolve species, and gain advantages during combat. Understanding item categories helps build stronger strategies and navigate the world more effectively.

## Item Categories

### **Healing Items**
Restore HP or cure status conditions.

- Potion  
- Super Potion  
- Hyper Potion  
- Full Heal  
- Antidote  

Healing items are essential for long journeys and tough battles.

### **Battle Items**
Used during combat to temporarily boost stats or influence turn outcomes.

- X Attack  
- X Defense  
- Guard Spec  
- Dire Hit  

These items can turn the tide of battle when used strategically.

### **Held Items**
Items Pokémon carry to gain passive benefits.

- **Leftovers** — Restores HP each turn  
- **Choice Band** — Boosts Attack but locks move choice  
- **Focus Sash** — Prevents one-hit knockouts  
- **Black Sludge** — Heals Poison-types, harms others  

Held items are a core part of competitive battling.

### **Evolution Items**
Trigger evolution for certain species.

- Fire Stone  
- Water Stone  
- Leaf Stone  
- Thunder Stone  

Example: Pikachu evolves into Raichu with a **Thunder Stone**.

### **Key Items**
Special items used for story progression or unique functions.

- Bicycle  
- Fishing Rods  
- Town Map  
- Exp. Share
