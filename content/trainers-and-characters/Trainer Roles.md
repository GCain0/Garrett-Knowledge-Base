# Trainer Roles

Trainers are individuals who form partnerships with Pokémon, guiding them through battles, training, and exploration.

## Types of Trainers

### **Casual Trainers**
People who raise Pokémon as companions or for small-scale battles.

### **Competitive Trainers**
Experts who focus on strategy, breeding, and high-level battling.  
See: [Advanced Battle Strategy](/battles-and-strategy/advanced-strategy)

### **Gym Leaders**
Powerful Trainers who specialize in a single type and oversee Gym challenges.

### **Champions**
The strongest Trainers in a region, serving as the final challenge.

### **Researchers**
Scientists who study Pokémon biology, habitats, and evolution.  
See: [What Is a Pokémon?](/pokemon-basics/what-is-a-pokemon)
